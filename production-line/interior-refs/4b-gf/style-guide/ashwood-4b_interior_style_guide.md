# Ashwood 4B interior style guide (concept)

concept visualisation, not survey-verified, not construction-ready, not certified CAD

Brochure interiors p15/p16, material board p17 and spec text p24 are PROJECT-level. None is verified as a 4B room; applying them to 4B rooms is an inference.

**Intent:** Warm-minimal 'quiet luxury': pale beige stone-look floors, white/greige textured walls, mid-to-dark walnut-tone veneer joinery, Calacatta-style marble/porcelain and travertine features, bronze metal trim, black linear ceiling lights, cream/taupe upholstery with muted sage and navy accents. Exterior tie-in: beige stone and bronze/timber slats from the 4B exterior concept set.

## Rules

- No people, no watermarks, no brochure logo/branding
- No fireplace/TV unless plan shows
- Windows/doors only where V4 plan shows openings
- Service rooms (pantry, laundry, K.store, maid room/bath, lift, MEP, RF pantry/powder) restrained: white paint, light porcelain, plain light veneer or white units, stainless fittings
- Wet areas follow p24: terrazzo-look or concrete-look porcelain; Calacatta only as one feature panel

Hex values are median pixel samples from brochure renders; they are not paint or product codes.
