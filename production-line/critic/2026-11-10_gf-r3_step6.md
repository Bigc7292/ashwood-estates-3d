## Builder's open items: rulings
1. **Pine vs p12 olive: DEFECT (G3).** Brochure p12 (`extracts/pages/page110-12.png`) is a real brochure render showing an olive tree beside the courtyard water, travertine walls and a timber deck. The pine comes only from the AI concept `EXT-4b-OUTDOOR-COURTYARD` (labelled "needs review"). Brochure outranks a generated concept for interior look (AGENTS.md interior step 1). Switch to an olive per p12, or add a mismatch-log row and get Colin's explicit call for the pine. Choosing one source silently breaks hard rule 2.
2. **Bench: SOURCED, PASS.** A timber bench is visible in the approved courtyard concept image, so it is not invented. Not on the V4 plan, so log it as an image-only item in the mismatch log. Keep it.
3. **Pool on lite water: DEFECT (G1).** `__FP3D_QA__.info().water` = `{mode:"lite", surfaces:[swimming-pool: lite]}`. The rule is that water surfaces use Water2. Make Water2 the default for the pool (keep `?water=lite` as the phone/perf fallback) and prove it with a two-frame diff like the courtyard one. The 2.8x SwiftShader frame cost is not a reason to break the rule; measure on a GPU/phone and report.
4. **p24 Calacatta vs travertine: DEFECT (G2), material mismatch.** p24 "Interior (Summary)": "Walls: Textured Paint / Feature Wall: Calacatta marble". p15-living shows a white veined Calacatta media wall. Make the living media/feature wall Calacatta. The entry and dining travertine walls are supported by p12 and the p17 swatch, so keep them, but list the p24 conflict in the mismatch log.
5. **Dress glass wardrobes: DEFECT (G4).** The V4 GF plan draws a joinery block on the dress room's east side (plan crop on `sbs_dress_rev.jpg`). Model that block as a glass-front, bronze-framed wardrobe (p15-dressing look) on that footprint only. No island: the plan does not draw one and the room is 1.10 m wide.
6. **Lift finish NOT FOUND: ACCEPTED.** Keep it labelled NOT FOUND, with a neutral finish. Not a fail.
