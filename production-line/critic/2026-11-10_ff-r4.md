## Defects
1. **The room labels are offset from their rooms (confirmed, critical).** The anchors are shifted west and north and compressed. In plan-derived metres (my measure, about ±0.3 m):
   - Master Suite: r4 (-1.24, 2.33), plan label about (1.9, 6.5), so off by (-3.1, -4.1) m. The label sits outside the building's west wall.
   - Bedroom 1: r4 (6.85, 2.51), plan about (11.5, 6.5), so off by (-4.6, -4.0).
   - Corridor: r4 (3.16, 9.05), plan about (7.1, 14.0), so off by (-4.0, -5.0). The label lands in M. Dressing.
   - Junior Suite: r4 (5.46, 12.18), plan about (9.9, 17.6), so off by (-4.5, -5.4). The label lands on the stair.
   - Labels for Master Suite, Master Dressing, Master Bath, Bath 2, Dressing and Bedroom 2 have x < 0, outside the envelope.
   - The best fit is plan = about 1.13-1.19 × r4 + (3.4 m, 3.8 m). So the anchors use a different scale and origin from the walls. r4 moved walls and anchors together by -1563 mm, which kept the error.
   - Fix: derive the anchors (and the label-centred room rects) with the same crop-px to mm transform as the walls (`origin_crop_px` [148, 164.5], 35.53 mm/px, then the -1563 mm envelope shift), from the SVG label text positions. Then re-check against this overlay.
2. **The room polygons are invented positions.** ROOMS are label-centred rectangles ("ASSUMED position"), so they inherit defect 1. The floor patches extend outside the west wall (2D plan x 250-330 px in `r4_2d_plan.png`). Fix: build the room polygons from the wall-enclosed regions with the doorways closed, as `STEPS.md` step 2 plans.
3. **The stair is too short and shifted.** The r4 stair rect is x 4.51-5.86, y 10.14-14.51 m (4.37 m run, 16 treads), assumed from GF. The plan stair runs from about y 9.3 to 15.8 m, about 1.5 m wide, with the flight drawn to the corridor. Fix: trace the FF stair from the plan. The prior "stair passes" is withdrawn at this precision.

Dressing, M. Bath, Bath 2, Dress, J. Dress, J. Bath) and facade glazing lines. They are in the source, so "NOT FOUND" is wrong. Fix: extract the door swings and glazing from the SVG.
