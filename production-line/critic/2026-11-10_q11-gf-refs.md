## Method
- For every room I put the builder's V4 plan crop (`cad-check/`, cut from `FP-4B-GF_V4_long7680.png`) beside the generated image. The side-by-sides are in `2026-11-10_q11-gf-refs/<room>_plancrop_vs_image.png`, and I opened all 15.
- Style: brochure refs `style-guide/refs/` (p15 living and dressing, p16 bedroom, p17 material board) and the p24 spec text as transcribed in `style-guide/ashwood-4b_interior_style_guide.md`.
- Orientation is inferred from the plan and the image, because the images carry no camera direction (`view-unassigned-001`).
