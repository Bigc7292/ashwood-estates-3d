# Critic report: walkable exterior v4 (exterior-garden-3d/4b/walkable/walkable_v4.html)

Date: 2026-11-10 (Asia/Dubai). Critic: lane 5 (independent). Builder: fixer lane (commit 3f20be6, lane/fixer). The file is the same blob (69c4668) on main, claude/rules-and-status, lane/fixer, lane/gf-interior and lane/roof-floor.
Labels: concept visualisation, not survey-verified, not construction-ready, not certified CAD.

**Verdict: CRITIC-FAIL. Score 3/10.** It loads offline with no page errors, the palms replaced the generic trees, and the pool sits in the CAD position. The look is far from the approved images, and the Water2 effect is not visibly working.

## Cameras checked
The viewer offers two cameras: the built-in `aerial` view (`?view=` default JSON, pos [-14,30,48]) and walk mode (start [-6,1.6,40], WASD and drag). It accepts free `?view=` JSON, so I also rendered front, back, left, right, top, aerial-back and a pool close-up, all at 1280x720 headless (SwiftShader), with the page reporting READY and no console errors.
- Side-by-sides (approved image | render): `2026-11-10_walkable-v4/w4_sbs_{aerial→OUTDOOR-SITE, walkstart→GARDEN-FRONT, front→ELEV-FRONT, back→ELEV-REAR, left→ELEV-LEFT, right→ELEV-RIGHT, aerial_back→GARDEN-BACK, pool→POOL}.png`, plus `w4_top.png` and `w4_contact.png` (all nine views).

## Water2 test
- The code builds `Water` (Water2) for the swimming pool when `flatwater` is not 1, with embedded normal maps, flowSpeed 0.02 and reflectivity 0.035.
- Test: a critic-only instrumented copy (`/workspace/critic-tools/walk/instr.html`, not committed; the only change keeps the render loop alive after 12 frames and exposes the camera). Two frames from the same pool camera, 6 s apart: **max per-pixel RGB difference 3/765, 0 pixels over 3.** The surface is a flat light-blue plane with no visible ripple or reflection of the house or palms (`2026-11-10_walkable-v4/water_t0_t1.png`).
- Caveats: in `?view=` mode the shipped page stops rendering after 12 frames, so stills can never animate. Under SwiftShader the loop ran at about 1 fps. A GPU check on Colin's PC would confirm, but at 0.035 reflectivity and this scale the effect is not visible even in still frames, which shows no reflection.

## Defects
1. **The Water2 effect is not visibly working.** The pool reads as a flat painted plane, with no ripples, no reflection of the facade or palms, and no change over 6 s (`water_t0_t1.png`). The reference POOL image shows a reflective surface with highlights. Fix: raise reflectivity (about 0.3-0.5), reduce scale so the ripples read at 9.5 m, check the normal-map load, and keep the render loop running (do not stop at 12 frames when the water is on screen).
2. **The aerial view has no context.** There is flat beige ground to the horizon and a grey RF box. OUTDOOR-SITE has a hedged plot, a paved drive, a lawn, neighbouring context and a travertine roof (`w4_sbs_aerial.png`). This is the prior v3 defect "flat grey ground" and it is still open. Fix: add a street, verge and neighbour massing, and a textured lawn and paving.
3. **The RF block is a bare grey box in every view** (aerial, back, left, right). M12 is open, but grey placeholders must be labelled in-scene. Use travertine at least.
4. **The rear facade glazing reads as grey smoked boxes.** ELEV-REAR shows full-height clear glazing with a lit interior, a glass balustrade, and timber and stone piers. The render shows opaque grey glass and a dark square at the FF centre (`w4_sbs_back.png` render x 755-785, y 110-140). M24 (the pier side) can't be judged until the glazing reads.
5. **The front view is behind the front-view lane.** It has no stone tower, roof cantilever, slat screen colour or hedges (`w4_sbs_front.png`). v4 predates passes 1-15. Do not port v4 forward; rebuild from the critic-passed front-view model.
6. **The palms are uniform.** They are all the same height band (6.5-7.9 m, NOT FOUND in source) and evenly ringed around the plot, including the side yards (`w4_top.png`). The references show 2-3 feature palms per view plus mixed trees and hedges. The sparse frond look is the same as in the Q4 finding.
7. **The terrace and pool edge are white and flat.** There is no coping mass, no step shadow and no lawn texture (`w4_sbs_pool.png`). This is the same as Q4's pool finding.
8. **The left and right elevations are blank walls with palms.** ELEV-LEFT/RIGHT (concept) show articulated stone and timber bands and openings. The render shows long flat travertine planes (`w4_sbs_left.png`, `w4_sbs_right.png`).
