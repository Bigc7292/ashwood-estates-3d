## What door.jpg shows

- Open portal. The opening is empty. No door leaves and no glazed double doors. A signal pole in front is street furniture, not the door.
- Lintel over the opening is carved **BARCLAY HOUSE**.
- Diamond transom: a glazed light above that lintel, leaded in a diamond lattice, in the pale stone frame. Bar count: NOT FOUND.
- Lion heads on the outer top corners of the pale stone surround, one each side.
- Floral vertical jambs: foliate carving down the outer uprights.
- Dark granite inner surround: a dark speckled border inside the pale frame, around the opening. Species not lab-tested. Portland stone is recorded for the ground floor up to first floor only (https://www.manchestervictorianarchitects.org.uk/buildings/barclays-warehouse-barclay-house-whitworth-street-west). That page does not name this inner stone or size the opening. Inner-stone name beyond the photo: NOT FOUND.
- Stairs rising inside the portal, away from the street. Treads light, risers darker. Tread count, rise, and going: NOT FOUND.
- Opening is rectangular, not a round arch. Outer frame is pale ashlar. A partial sign at the left edge is not the doorcase.

## Builder must match

Open portal with stairs rising inside. BARCLAY HOUSE lintel and diamond transom. Lion heads, floral vertical jambs, and dark granite inner surround.
