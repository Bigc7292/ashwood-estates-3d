# Realism checklist

Score only what is in the frame. 0 is broken, 5 is a clean diagram, 8 is a photograph with small tells, 10 is a photograph.

## Geometry (0–10)

- Edges that a person would touch are not razor sharp. A 2–4 mm bevel reads on stone and teak.
- Furniture has thickness. Cushions are not boxes. Legs meet the floor.
- Nothing intersects the pool coping, the glass, or the planting.
- Repeated parts are not clones at the same rotation.

## Materials (0–10)

- Stone is stone, not a stretched photo. Grout or a joint breaks the terrace into slabs.
- Water is darker in the deep end and mirrors the facade. A ripple is a distortion of that mirror, not a tiled normal with no reflection.
- Glass shows the place in front of it and, at dusk, a warm room behind it. It is not a colour gradient.
- Leaves have variation. A hedge is not one flat green.

## Light (0–10)

- One sun. Shadows agree with it.
- Dusk is a blue sky and a short orange band at the horizon, not an orange dome and not a hard stripe.
- Interior lamps are local. They do not paint the whole facade.
- The lawn takes the same light as the stone. Noon grass under a night sky is a fail.

## Reflections (0–10)

- A vertical window mirrors the space outside that wall.
- The pool mirrors the house.
- Metals are rough enough to blur the reflection. A perfect mirror on stone is wrong.

## Vegetation (0–10)

- Trunks meet the ground.
- Canopies are not spheres of one colour.
- Planters read as stone or metal with a plant, not a green column.

The critic returns the five scores, the average, and at most three fixes. Each fix names a number (height, hex, roughness, degrees, metres).
