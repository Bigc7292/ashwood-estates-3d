# Geometry

- Model the silhouette the camera sees. A rear terrace shot needs the pool edge, the frames, the furniture and the planting. It does not need door handles on the far side.
- Bevel only the edges in that shot: coping, table top, sofa arm. A 3 mm bevel is enough at 35 mm lens.
- The pool water is one plane on the waterline, normal up. The tile floor stays under it.
- Window glass is one plane per opening, on the outer face, normal pointing outside. Do not stack a second pane.
- A planter is a stone box with a lip. The plant sits in it, not through it.
- Keep object origins, but apply scale before any export to glTF.
- If Blender and the viewer disagree, the viewer frame wins, because that is what is delivered.
