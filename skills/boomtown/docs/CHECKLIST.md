# The checklist

The job description covers every house. The checklist covers one reference. Write it before the first line of code: what does this house stand on, in numbered lines a reader could check against the picture?

## Rules for writing one

- **10 to 12 lines.** Fewer misses features; more turns into a spec nobody grades.
- **Order:** silhouette and proportions first, then the roof, then the signature feature, then the faces and glass, then materials, then the outside, then the camera.
- **Every vague word gets a number.** "A steep roof" becomes "60°, ridge 9.2 m over a 9.2 m base".
- **Write "never" next to the default you are fighting.** "A glass gable to the apex, never a solid triangle with a window in it."
- **Proportions in metres, measured off the reference.** Footprint, eave, ridge, bay spacing.
- **The last line is always the look:** saturated tones and straight ink, at the axo and at the ground.

## Template

```
1. Silhouette: [the massing in one sentence]. Footprint [w x d] m, eave [h] m, ridge [h] m, pitch [deg]. Seen from [the hero side].
2. Ground: [plinth, platform, deck, piles], top at [h] m, [steps, ramps].
3. Structure: [frames, columns, ribs, bents], [how many] at [spacing] m. NEVER [the default: a solid box with no frame showing].
4. Roof: [type, material, seams or ribs every n m, overhangs]. [Skylights, vents, solar].
5. The signature feature: [the one thing a viewer remembers]. [Size in numbers].
6. Glass: [which faces, share of each face, mullion spacing, doors]. NEVER [glass as a flat blue sheet with no frame].
7. Solid faces: [cladding, boards, stone], [direction, spacing].
8. Inside, seen through the glass: [floors, a stair, two or three pieces of furniture].
9. Outside: [terrace, pool, shed, porch, fire pit], [sizes].
10. Planting: [trees, shrubs, a green roof], [how many, where].
11. The parts: [8 to 13 ids in build order]. The tour: [12 views].
12. Saturated three-tone colour and straight ink on every edge, at the axo and on the ground.
```

## Example: the A-frame cabin, 12 lines

```
1. A steep A-frame: base 9.2 m wide, ridge 9.2 m above the platform (63°), 10 m deep along the ridge. Seen from the front left, the glass gable to the eye.
2. A larch platform 19 x 15.4 m on screw piles, top 0.6 m; the cabin on its back two thirds, a porch in front, two steps down to the path.
3. Seven glulam ribs 200 x 320 at 1.67 m, landing on steel shoes; collar beams at the loft. NEVER a solid triangle with no ribs showing.
4. Roof: standing-seam steel to both slopes, seams every 450 mm, eave board, verge boards, a dark ridge cap; two roof windows on the west slope.
5. The glass gable: the whole front glazed to the apex, 6 mullions, 2 transoms clipped by the slope, a sliding door in the middle bay.
6. The back gable: vertical boards with one window at the loft.
7. A box dormer on the east slope: 3 m long, a window band, a flat steel roof, green cheeks.
8. A fieldstone chimney 1.2 x 1.2 m from the ground through the west slope to +7.6, a steel cap; the stove inside against it.
9. Inside through the glass: the loft deck at +3.9 with a mesh railing and a ladder, a sofa, a kitchen run at the back.
10. Outside: a cedar hot tub (2.3 m) on the porch, string lights sagging between four posts, a lean-to woodshed with stacked logs, a fire pit with log benches.
11. Parts: platform, frame, roof, gable, loft, dormer, chimney, porch, hottub, woodshed, clearing. Tour: AXO R, gable, loft, dormer, TOP, chimney, hot tub, FRONT, woodshed, roof, AXO L, GROUND.
12. Saturated timber orange, near-black roof, clear blue-green glass, straight ink, at the axo and on the ground.
```

Line 3's NEVER is there because the first frame without ribs read as a tent. Line 5's clipped transoms are there because rectangular panes poked through the roof.
