# Lessons

Every mistake below became one line in the job description. The next house starts with all of them. They come from building the deck house and its terminal over seven versions.

| What happened | What it looked like | The line it added |
|---|---|---|
| The header was rebuilt "in the spirit of" the mockup | Close, but every gap and size a little off | The terminal is copied exactly and is fixed; a house never restyles it |
| A logo and a gradient were redrawn in SVG | Near misses of the real pictures | Supplied pictures are embedded as pictures, never redrawn |
| Panels from other terminals were copied over | A collage of other people's designs | References are ideas only; every panel is redone in boomtown's own style |
| The house was drawn with a hand-drawn wobble and paper grain | Looked like a rendering fault, lines shimmered in motion | Lines straight, no wobble, no grain |
| A pastel wash over the model | A pale sketch; "don't make the house's colours pale" | Three flat tones per material, deep and saturated, never pastel |
| The tour flew to a view, eased in, parked, eased out | "The zoom thinks too long" | The house builds in 1 s, then one unbroken 24 s spline that never stops |
| Fine lines kept full strength when zoomed out | Muddy grey textures from afar | Main ink keeps 80% far off, fine lines step back to 30%, patterns fade before they get finer than a pixel |
| A pop-up card from another skill held the model | A postcard, not a terminal; "the card is not needed" | Keep the main screen, the model in the centre changing by frames with zoom |
| Panels filled with colour | They hid the house behind them | Side panels are clear glass, white text; the house may run under them |
| The house on a plain backdrop | Generic, flat | The house stands in a drawn meadow with trees, ferns, sky and a path |
| A QA pass over phones and screen sizes | Hours on a terminal that runs on one screen | Build for 1920 x 1080, check one or two stills, deliver |
| Purlins sat at the same offset as the roof skin | Orange stripes poking through the roof | Structure stays at least 0.1 m under the skin |
